Overview

SEPM (CS 331) blends software engineering practices—requirements, architecture, testing, CI/CD—with project management fundamentals—planning, estimation, risk, and team coordination. Students will complete a team project that simulates an industry workflow with modern tooling.

Course Project

Overview

Teams will design, implement, test, and deliver a small software product over multiple iterations. You are expected to practice requirements elicitation, architectural design, version control, automated testing, and continuous integration. Weekly sprint reviews will track progress.

Milestones

  1. Problem Statement & Requirements (Week 3)
  2. Architecture & Initial Prototype (Week 5)
  3. Iteration 1 — Core Features + Tests (Week 8)
  4. Iteration 2 — Extensions + CI (Week 11)
  5. Final Demo & Report (Week 14)

Deliverables

  • Team repo (GitHub) with Issues/Boards
  • Design document (architecture & rationale)
  • Automated test suite & CI configuration
  • Demo video (5–7 min) + Final report
